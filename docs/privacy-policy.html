Spectra Camera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Spectra Camera is designed to process all photo and video data locally on your device. We collect minimal data:

  • Camera & Microphone: Used to capture photos and record videos. All image processing happens entirely on your device.
  • Location (optional): If you grant permission, your GPS coordinates are embedded in photo EXIF metadata, just like the stock camera app. You can deny this permission and the app works normally without it.
  • Storage: Photos and videos are saved to your device's local storage (DCIM/Spectra).

2. AI Coaching (Optional)

Spectra Camera includes an optional AI coaching feature that, when enabled by you, sends a low-resolution snapshot to the Anthropic API for composition and lighting suggestions. This feature:

  • Is disabled by default
  • Requires you to provide your own API key
  • Only sends data when you explicitly enable it
  • Sends only low-resolution thumbnails, not full-resolution photos

Anthropic's data handling is governed by their own privacy policy.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information, analytics, or usage data
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or trackers
  • We do not upload your photos or videos to any server (except the optional AI coaching described above)
  • We do not have user accounts or registration

4. On-Device Processing

All AI features (scene detection, face detection, palm gesture recognition, depth estimation, image enhancement) run entirely on your device using TensorFlow Lite and MediaPipe models. No image data leaves your device for these features.

All photos and videos are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your files. Deleting the app removes all app data except photos saved to your gallery.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
